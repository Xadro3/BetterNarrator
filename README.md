# Better Narrator

An Android app that reads stories aloud from a selected area of your screen while you use your browser or reading app. Text recognition and AI voices run entirely on your device, with no account, subscription, or paid voice service.

## What works

- Select a reading area and narrate its text with offline Kitten Nano or Kokoro voices.
- Adjust the voice and reading speed.
- Control playback with floating Read, Pause, Next, and Off buttons.
- Scroll automatically and prepare the next screen while narration continues to reduce playback gaps.

Tested with Royal Road in Chrome on an Android emulator. Kindle, Google Play Books, and tap-based page turning still need testing. Protected content may block screen capture.

## Try the prerelease

Get the **prerelease APK for testing** from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Xadro3/BetterNarrator/releases). Requires Android 8 or newer.

Allow floating controls and page controls, tap **Turn on**, and approve screen sharing. Open your story, tap **Area** to select the text, then **Read**.
